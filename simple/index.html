The band

Bloom formed in 2017 when five schoolfriends from Belrose started writing music together. Two early EPs, Past Tense (2018) and In Passing (2020), built the band's reputation for pairing heavy riffs with big melodies.

The debut album Maybe In Another Life followed in 2024, with The Light We Chase arriving a year later. Since then the band have toured Australia and Europe, sharing stages with Silverstein, Chelsea Grin and Currents.

Latest release / Album / 2025

Bloom's second album, recorded in a few weeks with producer Sam Bassal. It moves from the heaviest songs the band have written to their softest.
