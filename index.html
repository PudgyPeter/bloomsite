The band

Bloom formed in 2017 when five schoolfriends from Belrose, on Sydney's northern beaches, started writing music together after hours in borrowed rooms.

Two early EPs, Past Tense (2018) and In Passing (2020), built the band's reputation for pairing bruising riffs with soaring melodies, and led to a deal with Greyscale Records. The debut album Maybe In Another Life followed in 2024, with The Light We Chase arriving a year later through Pure Noise Records.

Since then the band have toured Australia and Europe, sharing stages with Silverstein, Chelsea Grin and Currents, and played their first headline shows in Japan.

Album

The Light We Chase

Bloom's second album, written and recorded in a matter of weeks with producer Sam Bassal. Built around nostalgia for the band's shared school years in Belrose, it moves from the heaviest material they have recorded to their softest, with guest vocals from Patrick Miranda, Jack Bergin and Mikaila Delgado.
