The lineup

Members

Five schoolfriends from Sydney's northern beaches who started a band in 2017 and never stopped.

Member profiles

Jono Hawkey

Vocals

Frontman since day one, and the band's principal lyricist.

Jono is Bloom's vocalist and a founding member. He has fronted the band since it formed in 2017, when a group of schoolfriends from Belrose decided they wanted to make loud music together.

His voice spans the band's full range, from the screamed intensity of Withered to the restrained, almost-spoken passages on Tongue Tied. He writes most of the band's lyrics, often building them while the songs themselves are still being written, and much of Bloom's catalogue returns to the same territory: grief, nostalgia and the disappointment of expectations.

He has appeared on every Bloom release to date, from the 2017 debut single Cure Me through to The Light We Chase.

Essential listening

  • Withered
  • In Passing
  • Siren Song

Jarod McLaren

Guitar

Guitarist, songwriter, and the band's clean voice.

Jarod is a founding guitarist and one of Bloom's main songwriters. Alongside his guitar work he contributes clean vocals, trading lines with Jono on tracks such as Bound To Your Whispers, where the shift between the two voices carries much of the song's tension.

His playing has shaped Bloom's gradual move from post-hardcore weight toward the wider, more melodic sound of the recent records, bringing atmosphere and restraint as often as riffs.

Outside the band he works in visual effects, with credits including Peter Rabbit, Captain Marvel and The Batman.

Essential listening

  • Bound To Your Whispers
  • June
  • Tongue Tied

Oliver Butler

Guitar

Founding guitarist and the band's rhythmic anchor.

Oliver has played guitar in Bloom since the band's earliest sessions in Sydney, building the twin-guitar framework with Jarod that has carried every release.

Where Jarod tends to write the melodic and textural parts, Oliver's guitar work supplies the weight underneath them. His playing on Maybe In Another Life and The Light We Chase is a large part of why the band can move between hardcore aggression and wide, open choruses without losing either.

He is a founding member and has appeared on all of Bloom's recordings.

Essential listening

  • Keep You
  • Glen Street
  • Out Of Reach

Andrew Martin

Bass

Founding bassist and half of the rhythm section.

Andrew is Bloom's bassist and a founding member, holding down the low end since the band formed in 2017. He also shares songwriting credits across the catalogue.

His bass anchors the band's heavier material, giving the downtuned riffs on Maybe In Another Life their punch while keeping the melodic sections grounded. In a band that shifts between extremes, that consistency is what holds the songs together.

He has played on every Bloom release, from Past Tense through to The Light We Chase.

Essential listening

  • Daylight
  • Act II
  • Cold

Jack Van Vliet

Drums

Drummer, founding member, and the band's engine room.

Jack is Bloom's drummer and a founding member, driving the band from behind the kit since 2017. His playing is the constant against which the rest of Bloom's dynamics are measured, easing back for the acoustic passages and pushing hard when the songs open up.

He is also one of the band's most forthcoming voices in interviews, having spoken about the rapid, focused sessions that produced The Light We Chase and about the band's determination to keep taking risks rather than repeat themselves.

He has toured with Bloom across Australia, Europe and Japan, and played on every release since the band's formation.

Essential listening

  • Forget Me Not
  • Only Sky
  • The Works Of You
